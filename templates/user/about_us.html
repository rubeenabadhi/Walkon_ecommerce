Who We Are

WalkOn was born in Kerala with a simple belief: great footwear should be comfortable, stylish, and affordable — without compromise.

From our small workshop in Kochi, we have grown into a trusted name for quality shoes that suit Indian feet, Indian weather, and Indian lifestyles. Whether you're walking to college, heading to work, or stepping out for a weekend vibe — WalkOn is with you every step.

Our Mission

To deliver footwear that combines Indian craftsmanship with modern design — so every Indian can walk confidently and comfortably.

Our Vision

To become the most loved footwear brand in every Indian household — one comfortable step at a time.
